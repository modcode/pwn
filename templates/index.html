How to export from Google Maps / Google Takeout ▸
  1. Go to takeout.google.com and sign in.
  2. Click Deselect all, then scroll to Maps (your places) and check it.
  3. Choose Export once → any file format → Create export.
  4. Download the archive and find Saved Places.json inside the Maps folder.
  5. Upload that file here — your notes and locations will appear on the globe.

CSV tip: If you're exporting a specific list, the CSV should have at minimum Title, Note, URL, and optionally Latitude/Longitude columns.
